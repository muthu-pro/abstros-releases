### Added
- **Abstros updates itself.** It checks for a new version once a day and offers it in a window with what's new. Installing asks for your admin password, as the installer does, and Abstros reopens once on the new version. Choose Abstros › Check for Updates… (also in the menu bar icon's menu) to look now. Updates are signed: Abstros installs only a package signed with the Abstros update key and notarized by Apple.
- **Updates never interrupt a session.** While you're on a call, recording or live, a new version is only noted ("Update to 0.4.x…" in the menus, a banner in Settings) and offered once you're free. An update you accepted mid-session waits before it quits and reopens Abstros.
- **Update settings** in Settings › System › Updates: check automatically, download in the background, Stable or Beta versions, when it last checked, and Check Now.

### Changed
- **Branded installers.** The installer shows the Abstros mark in its sidebar, in light and dark, with restyled welcome and finish pages. The finish page says future updates come through the app.
- **Branded microphone packages.** The Abstros Microphone install and uninstall packages that Settings opens are now proper installers with the same branding and short explanations, not bare packages.
- **One launch after an update.** When the update installs from the app, the installer leaves reopening Abstros to the updater, so it opens once.
